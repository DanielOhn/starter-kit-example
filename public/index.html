    Questions

    Before you book

    Do you need to use my water or power?

    No. The van carries its own fresh water tank, generator and heater, so we're fully self-contained. All we need is a legal place to park within about 50 feet of your door.

    How long does an appointment take?

    A full groom runs about 60–90 minutes depending on coat and size. Bath & brush is usually 45 minutes, and a nail trim is closer to 15.

    Do I have to be home?

    Someone over 18 needs to be there to hand off and pick up your pet. You don't have to stand outside with us — most people go back in and we knock when we're done.

    My dog is anxious or senior. Is that a problem?

    It's the main reason people call us. One groomer, no other animals, no cage, no kennel dryers, and we stop the moment your pet says it's had enough.

    What does it actually cost?

    Listed prices are starting points for small, well-maintained coats. Larger breeds and heavy matting cost more — we'll quote the exact number when we confirm, before any work begins.
